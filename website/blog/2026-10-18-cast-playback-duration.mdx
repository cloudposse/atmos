A deployment can take thirty seconds to finish even when a demo only needs four
seconds to show its progress. Speeding up the entire recording also rushes the
commands and the pauses that help viewers follow along. Atmos now supports
[playback groups](/steps/type/cast#set-a-segments-playback-duration) to set the
recorded duration of just the steps you choose.

<!--truncate-->

## The Problem

Real infrastructure demos include time spent waiting for providers and services.
Those waits belong in the execution, but they can dominate a short recording.
Removing frames loses the progress that explains what happened.

## The Fix

A playback group preserves every captured event and scales its timestamps to fit
an exact duration. Typing and reading pauses outside the group retain their
original timing. The command still runs normally, with real timeouts and live
output during recording.

## How to Use It

Inside a cast session, wrap Enter and the completion wait in a group:

```yaml
- type: write
  text: atmos terraform deploy --stack dev --all
- type: playback
  duration: 4s
  steps:
    - type: key
      key: enter
    - type: wait
      regex: '(?s)Apply .*completed.*> '
      timeout: 90s
- type: pause
  duration: 3s
```

The four seconds begin before Enter and end when the completion wait matches.
The following three-second pause gives viewers time to read the result at the
returned prompt. Elapsed times printed by the command still show the actual
execution time; they are part of the preserved output.

The duration must be positive. Shorter segments stretch to fit, and playback
groups cannot nest. Groups also work around ordinary steps in a
[steps-mode cast](/steps/type/cast).
