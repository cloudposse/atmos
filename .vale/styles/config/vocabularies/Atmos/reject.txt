# Words to avoid in Atmos technical writing, in favor of plain equivalents.
# A small, generic plain-language list of our own choosing, not a
# reproduction of the ASD-STE100 controlled vocabulary.

# Do not leave a bare "#" on a line of its own in this file. Vale strips a
# comment only when the "#" is followed by text. A lone "#" falls through
# and compiles into a live pattern that matches every literal hash in the
# docs: issue shorthand such as owner/repo#1234, anchor links, and shell
# comments inside prose. Separate paragraphs with a blank line instead.

# Each entry is prefixed with (?i) because Vale's Vocab entries are
# case-sensitive by default, and these words appear capitalized at the
# start of a sentence just as often as lowercase mid-sentence.

(?i)leverage
(?i)synergy
(?i)seamlessly
(?i)robust
(?i)simply
(?i)obviously
